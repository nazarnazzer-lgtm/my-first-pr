Client
National Sawdust
Year

My role
Art Director
Scope
Season campaign, artist posters, lobby screens

The brief

Twenty-seven shows with nothing in common but the room.

Opera, string quartets, folk, spatial audio, album releases, a Maqam opera in Arabic. National Sawdust's twelfth season had to read as one season without flattening a single one of them.

The insight

The barrier to experimental music is not taste, it is permission. People are not staying away because they tried it and disliked it. They stay away because they assume they need to know something first.

The idea

Come as you are.

27

Shows in one season, all on the same system.
