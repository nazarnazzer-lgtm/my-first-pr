National Yiddish Theatre
Folksbiene, Identity Refresh

In review with the client

The marks are with the Folksbiene while they choose. Nothing from the round is published here.

The full set is shown in person.

Client
National Yiddish Theatre Folksbiene
Year
2026
My role
Art Director
Scope
Identity refresh, logo system

The brief

One mark, for a name that runs five words across two alphabets.

The Folksbiene has programmed continuously since 1915. The identity carries the English and the Yiddish without either turning into a subtitle, and it has to hold at both ends of the range the company actually works at: an Ahmanson-scale poster and a line in a mailing-list email.

The problem

An institution this old is usually read as an archive. The refresh had to keep what 111 years earns and still sit in front of a season that programmes new work every year. Heritage without the museum.

The approach

Rather than one recommendation, I built the refresh as a set of distinct directions, each taken far enough to be judged where it will live: on a poster, on a ticket, and at the size a phone gives it. The company is choosing between them now.

Where it stands

With the client. I don't publish a mark a client hasn't picked, so this page carries the thinking and none of the artwork. The season campaign for the same company is published in full.
