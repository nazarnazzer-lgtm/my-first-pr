National Yiddish Theatre
Folksbiene, Identity Refresh

Client
National Yiddish Theatre Folksbiene
Year
2026
My role
Art Director
Scope
Identity refresh, mark options

The brief

One mark, for a name that runs five words across two alphabets.

The Folksbiene has programmed continuously since 1915. The identity carries the English and the Yiddish without either turning into a subtitle, and it has to hold at both ends of the range the company actually works at: an Ahmanson-scale poster and a line in a mailing-list email.

The problem

An institution this old is usually read as an archive. The refresh had to keep what 111 years earns and still sit in front of a season that programmes new work every year. Heritage without the museum.

The approach

Rather than one recommendation, I drew the refresh as nine distinct options, each taken far enough to be judged where it will live: on a poster, on a ticket, and at the size a phone gives it.

The options

01

Each direction is drawn in every colourway it was tested in, because a mark that only holds in one colour is not a system yet. All of them carry the English and the Yiddish together rather than treating either as a translation of the other.
