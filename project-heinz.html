Heinz
Hot Breakfast Tour

Brand
Heinz (spec)
Year
2026
My role
Art Director, Designer
Scope
Self-initiated. Campaign, packaging, web

The brief

Put a jalapeño ketchup on the American breakfast table.

Not as a novelty on the shelf but as a staple, the bottle that comes out every morning. Heat is already on the American breakfast table, in the eggs and the hash and the hot sauce beside them. The ketchup had not followed it there.

The idea

The one thing at every breakfast.

The device

An American couple eats breakfast around the world, and the campaign is built as a tour: a Heinz passport, boarding passes, stamps, a route across the map. Every country brings a different breakfast. The bottle is the only thing on every table.

On the road

London, Mumbai, Mexico City. The postcard does the work a location caption would have done, and the bottle turns up in every frame without being explained.

The tour is what gives it somewhere to go. Every new country is another breakfast, another stamp in the passport and the same bottle on the table, so the campaign can keep running without needing a new idea each time.
