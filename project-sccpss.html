Before and after. The oak stayed; what it is made of changed.

Client
Savannah-Chatham County Public Schools
Year
2026
My role
Designer
Scope
SCADpro. Brand system, icons, pattern, applications

The problem

A district brand is not used by designers. It is used by thousands of people who need a logo for a flyer by Friday.

The old mark was a drawn oak in an oval with the district name on a banner under it. It reads as a county seal, which is accurate about the institution and says nothing about the children in it. It also only worked at one size, in one arrangement, with no answer for a navigation icon, a department, a bus, a badge or a square social post. Every one of those gaps was being filled locally, by whoever needed it, in whatever was to hand.

The idea

Keep the oak. Savannah is a city of them and the district had a century of equity in the shape. But rebuild it out of figures, so the tree is made of the people in it, and set the initials as four stacked blocks that work as a square, a stamp or a favicon.

Then build everything else out of that one decision: the same line weight and the same round terminals become an icon set, a pattern, and a lockup every department can put its own name into.

01

The tree, the stacked initials, and the horizontal lockup for places where a square will not fit. One system, three ratios.

The horizontal lockup, both ways round.

Web navigation

02

The district site is where most parents actually meet the brand. Nine icons cover the things people arrive looking for, drawn on the same grid and the same stroke as the mark so the set reads as one hand.

Services

03

The second half of the set names what the district actually offers. Same grid, same weight, but these carry a programme rather than a link.

Six programmes, same hand.

The pattern

04

Cut the terminals off the mark and the leftover curve tiles. It gives the district a surface for the things a logo cannot carry on its own: an envelope, a lanyard, a wall, the back of a bus.

The pattern at scale, and the variations for sub-brands.

The departments

05

Twelve departments needed to sign their own work without twelve logos. The lockup takes a name and holds everything else still.

One lockup, a different name in it each time.

In the world

06

Print, digital and environment. The test of a district system is whether the same four blocks still read on a lanyard at arm’s length and on a building from across a car park.

Print and merchandise, posted as a set.

The site and the feed, running the same icon set.

Designer on the SCADpro team for Savannah-Chatham County Public Schools: the mark, the icon set, the pattern and how they carry across the applications above. What I would keep from it is the constraint: a system for a public district is only as good as the worst person who has to use it at four o’clock on a Friday.
