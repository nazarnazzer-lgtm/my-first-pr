I don't spend much time on the edge. If something feels right, I commit and figure it out in motion.

That instinct comes from swimming. Showing up early, performing tired, knowing that once you're in you keep moving or you sink. Ten years of it made me a two-time NAIA All-American. It's the same instinct that took me from Moscow to the U.S. to study advertising at SCAD with no perfect plan and no return date.

Since then I've worked on brand and campaign work for National Sawdust, National Yiddish Theatre Folksbiene and Common Senses Festival, and I lead brand direction at Magnus. Work I've made has been featured at The Grammys and New York SummerStage.

Most of it was made without the resources to buy attention, which is the fastest way to learn that the idea has to carry the work. I'd rather bring that discipline to a brief with real resources behind it than the other way round.
