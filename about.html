I'm an art director building brand systems and campaigns across culture, fashion and the public sector.

I'm from Moscow. I came to the U.S. for school without much of a plan and ended up staying. Ten years of competitive swimming got me through it: two-time NAIA All-American, a couple of school records, and a lot of very early mornings. I graduated from SCAD in 2026 with a BFA in Advertising and Branding.

Since then I've worked on projects for National Sawdust, National Yiddish Theatre Folksbiene and Common Senses Festival, and I run brand direction at Magnus. Work I've made has been featured at The Grammys and New York SummerStage. A SCAD project I worked on, the NR-560, won a European Product Design Award in 2026.

Most of it was made without media spend behind it, where an idea earns attention or nothing happens. That is the harder school, and it is what I bring to a bigger shop: thinking already built to work without scale. Scale only makes it travel further.
